    RFC 2119: You MUST package your stuff properly

    • One closed, sturdy container per label - box, crate or case. No loose items, no open bags, no "it's just a cable".
    • It must survive being stacked, pushed and driven 900 km. Pad fragile content and close the lid for real (tape, straps, latches).
    • Each container needs its own registration and its own labels. Several containers = add several containers in the list on the left.
    • List what is inside, precisely. Customs checks the content against the Carnet ATA, so "box with phones" is not enough.
    • Stick the label on a side that stays visible when stacked - not on the top, not on the bottom.

    Improperly packaged frames may be dropped at ingress by the van crew.

    Nothing is uploaded: this page runs entirely in your browser and has no server behind it. The e-mail in step 4 is the only way your registration reaches us.

    Fill out the form one container at a time

    FRAME The container itself

    Weight and size are needed for load planning, the value of the content for the Carnet ATA (customs paper for crossing the border).

    DST ADDR Where it goes
    SRC ADDR Where it is picked up
    PAYLOAD What is inside, item by item

    Customs needs a precise list for the Carnet ATA. "Telefonkiste" is not enough: list every kind of item with brand and model, how many there are, and what one piece is worth. For example:

    4 × Yealink T46G, CHF 80
6 × Gigaset DECT Handset mit Ladeschale, CHF 50
1 × Bakelit Telefon (1950), CHF 50
1 × Logitech Maus/Tastatur, CHF 15
2 × LED Lichterkette, CHF 10
    QtyDescription (brand, model)CHF / pieceTotal

    Total value: CHF 0

    A single big item is simply a list with one line. Rough estimates of the value are fine. The list is printed on its own sheet that goes inside the container.

    QoS Class How important is it that this arrives?

    If the van is full, frames with the lowest priority are dropped first: they stay at the aggregation point and are not picked up. Be fair - not everything is EF.

    Reply-To Who to call about this

    Only the name is required. Please add at least one way to reach you if you can - Matrix or phone also work before the event. This will be printed on the label.

    Options Variable length, additional notes

    Print the pages 2 labels + payload list

    Page 1 is the outbound label: attach it now. Page 2 is the return label: put it inside the container or tape it behind the first one, and attach it in Hamburg before teardown. Page 3 (and following, for long lists) is the payload list: put it inside the container, on top of the content.

    E-mail the info to yourself, CC info@luxeria.ch

    Please send the transport sheet to yourself and put info@luxeria.ch in CC.

    • Backup: if a label gets lost or torn off, we can still tell whose container it is - and so can you.
    • Planning: Luxeria collects all sheets into one list with value, weight and dimensions. That list is what the van crew uses for load planning and for the Carnet ATA.

    No mail program on this device, or the draft comes up empty or cut off (this can happen with long lists)? Use "Copy sheet as text" and paste it into a mail to yourself, CC info@luxeria.ch. Send one mail per container.
