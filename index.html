VAN-Transport-Protocol

Register your goods for the Transalpin van and print the labels for your container.

Read the intro

RFC 2119: You MUST package your stuff properly

  • One closed, sturdy container per label - box, crate or case. No loose items, no open bags, no "it's just a cable".
  • It must survive being stacked, pushed and driven 900 km. Pad fragile content and close the lid for real (tape, straps, latches).
  • Each container needs its own registration and its own labels. Several containers = fill in the form several times.
  • Stick the label on a side that stays visible when stacked - not on the top, not on the bottom.

Improperly packaged frames may be dropped at ingress by the van crew.

Nothing is uploaded: this page runs entirely in your browser and has no server behind it. The e-mail in step 4 is the only way your registration reaches us.

Fill out the form one form per container

DST ADDR Where it goes
SRC ADDR Where it is picked up
PAYLOAD What is inside

A rough estimate of the value is fine. We need it, together with weight and size, for load planning and to file the Carnet ATA (customs paper for crossing the border).

QoS Class How important is it that this arrives?

If the van is full, frames with the lowest priority are dropped first: they stay at the aggregation point and are not picked up. Be fair - not everything is EF.

Reply-To Who to call about this

Only the name is required. Please add at least one way to reach you if you can - Matrix or phone also work before the event. This will be printed on the label.

Options Variable length, additional notes

Print the pages 2 × A4: way there and way back

Page 1 is the outbound label: attach it now. Page 2 is the return label: put it inside the container or tape it behind the first one, and attach it in Hamburg before teardown.

E-mail the info to yourself, CC info@luxeria.ch

Labels printed? Then one thing is left - without this mail we do not know your item exists.

Please send the transport sheet to yourself and put info@luxeria.ch in CC.

  • Backup: if a label gets lost or torn off, we can still tell whose container it is - and so can you.
  • Planning: Luxeria collects all sheets into one list with value, weight and dimensions. That list is what the van crew uses for load planning and for the Carnet ATA.

No mail program on this device? Use "Copy sheet as text" and paste it into a mail to yourself, CC info@luxeria.ch. Send one mail per container.
